Our Story

Founded in 2019, King Design Collective emerged from a vision to merge premium craftsmanship with cutting-edge AR technology. What started as a small design studio has grown into a global brand recognized for innovation in both footwear and interactive experiences.

Our flagship innovation — the AR card-battler integration — allows customers to bring their sneakers to life through augmented reality, unlocking exclusive digital collectibles and gameplay. This fusion of physical and digital has redefined what it means to own a pair of sneakers.

We believe in creating products that tell stories, and in crafting experiences that linger long after the purchase. Every pair of King Design Collective sneakers is a gateway to adventure.

— Alex Chen, Founder & Creative Director
