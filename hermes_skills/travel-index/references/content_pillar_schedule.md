# The Vagary Index — Content Pillar Schedule

## Overview
Three rotating pillars keep the channel balanced between growth, authority, and revenue. Each pillar maps to a specific agent duty and output format.

## Pillar 1: Solo Travel
Focus: One traveler, maximum freedom.
 cadence: 2x per week
 Formats:
  - 60s TikTok/Reels: "Solo in [Country]" — single stunning shot, voiceover tip
  - Carousel: "5 Things I Wish I Knew Before Solo Traveling to [Country]"
  - Long-form YouTube: "Solo Traveler’s Guide to [Country]" — 8-12 min
 Agent duty: Write scripts, shoot lists, and captions under the Solo Travel angle.
 Hook library:
  - "I spent 7 days alone in [Country] and here's what no one tells you."
  - "Solo travel in [Country] is cheaper than you think."
  - "The one mistake every solo traveler makes in [Country]."

## Pillar 2: Couples Travel
Focus: Shared experiences, romantic logistics.
 cadence: 2x per week
 Formats:
  - 60s TikTok/Reels: "Couples Trip: [Country]" — split-screen itinerary moment
  - Carousel: "Our Perfect 7 Days in [Country] as a Couple"
  - Long-form YouTube: "[Country] for Couples — Full Itinerary" — 10-15 min
 Agent duty: Generate couples itinerary snippets, caption angles, and thumbnail concepts.
 Hook library:
  - "We tested the most romantic itinerary in [Country] so you don't have to."
  - "Couples travel hack that saved us $1,200 in [Country]."
  - "The hidden gem in [Country] no Instagrammer will show you."

## Pillar 3: Guide Promotions
Focus: Authority positioning and direct response.
 cadence: 1x per week
 Formats:
  - 90s TikTok/Reels: "Why We Mapped All 250 Countries" — brand origin story
  - Static post: "The 250-Country Guide is LIVE" + checklist snippet
  - Long-form YouTube: "How to Plan a Trip to Any Country in 10 Minutes" — product demo
 Agent duty: Draft promo scripts, social proof snippets, and CTA variants.
 Hook library:
  - "We spent 2 years mapping every country so you can travel smarter."
  - "The only travel resource you'll ever need."
  - "From couch to coastline — this is your blueprint."

## Thumbnail Rules
- Human face + landmark in every thumbnail
- Text overlay max 4 words, bold yellow on black
- Consistent crown watermark in bottom right, 10% opacity

## Caption Structure
1. Hook (line 1)
2. Context (1-2 lines)
3. Value bullet or single tip
4. CTA: "Grab the full 250-Country Guide in bio"
