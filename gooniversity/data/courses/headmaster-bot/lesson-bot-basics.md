# Bot Basics

> **Course:** HeadMaster Bot · **Lesson:** 1 of 3

## Meeting Your Digital Goon

Right, first rule of HeadMaster Bot — it's not gonna read your mind, so you need to learn its language. Start by hitting up @HeadMasterGooner_Bot on Telegram and dropping the `/start` command, which basically says "oi, I'm here to play." From there the menu pops up and you can see what's on offer, but the real moves are in the slash commands. `/rank` tells you where you sit on the leaderboard, `/daily` claims your gooner points for showing up, `/stats` breaks down your activity across the server, and `/help` brings up the full command list without you having to sound someone out. The bot's set up to reward engagement, so the more you use it, the more it works for you.

## Command Breakdown

Here's the gooner command cheat sheet — bookmark this in your brain because muscle memory saves lives. `/daily` gives you a chunk of points just for being active once per day, and it resets at the same time for everyone so you'll catch people racing to claim it. `/ping` shows you the bot's response time and proves it's alive, which sounds silly but you'd be amazed how many gooners think the bot's broken when really they're just impatient. `/inventory` shows you all the silly hats and titles you've unlocked, and `/equip` puts on whatever cosmetic you fancy. The `/quest` command is where it gets spicy — it rotates every twelve hours and gives you bonus points for doing specific server activities like welcoming new members or sharing content.

## Pro Tips for Lazy Gooners

The HeadMaster Bot rewards consistency, not cramming, so set yourself a daily alarm to hit `/daily` and your future self will thank you when you're not scrambling to catch up. You can also chain commands — `/daily` followed by `/quest` takes two seconds and doubles your morning point haul. Don't ignore the achievement notifications either; the bot drops little easter eggs and secret titles that only show up after you do something unexpected, like being online during a server milestone or being one of the first ten people to react to a specific message. The bot tracks everything, and a lot of those hidden achievements are pure chaos — exactly the kind of thing gooners thrive on. Learn the bot, use the bot, become the bot.
