# Leaderboards

> **Course:** HeadMaster Bot · **Lesson:** 2 of 3

## How Points Actually Work

Right gooner, you wanna climb the leaderboard — first rule is you gotta understand how the points machine actually grinds. Every interaction with the server drips into your score: posting a message in the main channel gives you a base 5 points, but only if it's not pure spam — the bot's got a 30-word minimum before it even considers counting. Reply to someone and you're looking at another 10 for engagement, reacting to a message is worth 3 points each, and voice chat participation is the big daddy at 20 points per ten minutes. The bot also runs random multipliers — double point weekends happen every month, and during special events you can see fivefold bonuses just for being in the right channel at the right time. The thing is, the bot caps you out after about 2000 points a day, which means no amount of spam will push you past the wall.

## Ranking Up the Server

Your rank isn't just flex value — it unlocks real perks as you climb the greasy pole. Hit Copper tier and you get a custom role color. Silver gets you into the voice channels without waiting in queue. Gold unlocks exclusive emoji and the ability to pin your own messages for five minutes. Platinum is where it gets spicy — you get a special ping role that the mods use for beta testing new server features, and you can request custom bot commands that the public can't touch. Diamond and above? Those gooners basically run the server themselves — they get to propose new channels, suggest rule changes, and their votes count double in community polls. The bot makes it obvious what your next threshold is, so you're never guessing whether you're 50 points or 5000 away from the next rank.

## The Meta Grind

Here's the real goon move: don't just chase points blindly, ride the weekly meta like a surfer hits a wave. The leaderboard resets every month, but within that month the bot introduces weekly challenges that are worth fat stacks of bonus points — things like "most original memes posted" or "best squad interaction streak." These rotate and they're announced every Monday, so check the announcements channel and pivot your usual server behaviour to match. A lot of gooners sit in the top 10 spots by accident because they were already doing the meta behaviour before it got gamified. Also, don't sleep on the co-op bonuses — if you and your squad-mates all hit your daily targets, you each get a 25% bonus at the end of the day. The system was built for pack hunters, not lone wolves.
