# Fusion Flow

> **Course:** Aura Trinity Mastery · **Lesson:** 2 of 3

## Tap to Fuse

Fusion Flow is the bread-and-butter rhythm that every gooner needs to lock into muscle memory, so let's walk through it slow. It starts with the NFC tap — you lay your champion card on the pad and hold it there for exactly one beat, until you feel the little haptic bump. Don't yank it off straight away, that cancels the read. Let the app confirm the champion's details, then lift clean. The verify step is where gooners mess up: a lot of you are staring at your phone while the pad chugs, and if the connection drops mid-read you get a phantom card that fuses as "unknown." Always wait for the green check. Only then do you slide into the fuse menu and confirm the merge — if anything looks off, cancel and re-tap. Rushing this gets you stuck with a dud champion and a 10-minute cooldown.

## Resource Management

Fusion isn't free, and the game will happily eat your whole mana bar if you're not paying attention. Every fuse costs a chunk of resonance dust that refills slowly over time, or faster if you've got the daily bonus active. The smart gooner queues up their fusions during the double-dust window that runs every evening — that's when you burn through your backlog of dupes instead of hoarding them. Also keep your junk champions at level 1 when you're stockpiling for a fusion push, because the dust cost scales with the card's current level. Nothing worse than dumping 300 dust on a level-15 card you were planning to eat anyway.

## The Reset Window

Here's the juicy bit: after every successful fusion the board flashes "Reset Available" for three seconds if you're on streak three or higher. That's your cue to chain into the next tap without leaving the screen, which keeps your combo meter spinning and your dust efficiency through the roof. Miss the window and you're back to square one. So here's the gooner move: line up your next three champions before you start the sequence, keep your phone on a stable surface so you're not fumbling taps, and never, ever fuse during a lag spike. The Fusion Flow is a dance — smooth and deliberate wins every time.
