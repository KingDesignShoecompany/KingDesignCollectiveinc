# Convergence-50

> **Course:** Aura Trinity Mastery · **Lesson:** 3 of 3

## Unlocking the 50 Threshold

Alright gooners, this is where the training wheels come off — Convergence-50 is the first real breakpoint that separates the board-readers from the button-mashers. To unlock the 50% synergy you need all three Trinity stones active on your board simultaneously, each sitting at minimum tier 2. Sounds simple, but the catch is that the game won't register the threshold until the overlap is sustained for a full two-second tick. That means no rapid-tap shenanigans — plant your stones and leave them. A lot of fresh goons panic-tap during the countdown and break their own field. Breathe. Hold. Watch the corner of your screen go from dim white to a solid glow, that's your 15% secondary stat bump locked in.

## Reward Geometry

The reward geometry at 50 is deliberately awkward, which is the devs' way of telling you "this is a foundation, not the top floor." Your main stat gets a modest 8% lift, but the secondary — the one shared across all three stones — jumps a clean 15%. That's why gooner builds that hit 50 early feel noticeably beefier even if the raw numbers look modest on paper. The geometry also opens up a new slot in your talent grid, usually one that gives you a small energy refund on fusion. It's not glamorous, but that refund is what lets you push into 75 without starving your reserves. Think of 50 as "the gooner tax" — pay it now or pay more later.

## Pushing to 75

Once you're comfortable riding the 50 wave, the natural next step is hunting 75 — same rules, tighter timing window, and you'll want to have a fourth stone ready to rotate in for the big overlap. The gooner shortcut here is to pre-charge your lowest-tier stone while you're holding the 50 overlap, so the moment the tick lands you're already drifting toward resonance. Don't sleep on the cooldown either: after a 50 lock resolves you get a brief grace period where the fields hold even if you swap cards, so use that to set up your 75 push instead of mashing the next tap. Convergence-50 isn't the endgame, but it's the handshake every gooner needs to master before they can flex at the real table.
