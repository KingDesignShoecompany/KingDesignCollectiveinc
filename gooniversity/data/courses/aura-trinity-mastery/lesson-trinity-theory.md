# Trinity Theory

> **Course:** Aura Trinity Mastery · **Lesson:** 1 of 3

## The Three Stones

Right, gather 'round gooners — Trinity Theory is where you stop guessing and start actually piloting. The Trinity stones are three aura-aligned cores that sit underneath your champion board: Mind (blue), Body (orange), and Spirit (green). You don't just slap them on the board and hope — each stone projects a field that overlaps with the others, and where they overlap is where the real stats live. Mind gives you spell power and crit rate, Body dishes out raw HP and defense, and Spirit pumps energy recharge and healing bonus. On their own each stone is decent, but stack 'em right and suddenly your mid-tier champion is hitting numbers that'd make a gacha dev cry.

## Convergence Thresholds

Here's the twist that catches fresh gooners out: the stones don't scale linearly. They snap into new tiers at convergence thresholds — 50%, 75%, and 100% overlap. At 50% you get your first synergy bonus, usually a flat 15% bump to the shared secondary stat. Hit 75% and you unlock the "Resonance" modifier, which doubles the effect of the smallest stone. By 100% you're in Pure Form territory, where all three fields merge and you unlock a unique aura trait that only shows up when all three stones are perfectly aligned. This is why a lot of gooners will sacrifice raw power on a single stone to push the triple overlap over the hundo mark — the payoff is that much cleaner.

## Reading Your Board

The trick is spotting convergence before you commit your taps. Pull up your board view and look for the little intersection markers — they glow faintly at 50%, pulse at 75%, and outright flash when you're sitting on a 100% Pure Form. New goons often chase the shiny individual stone bonuses, but the real flex is holding two stones at 75% and one at 50%, because that 75% resonance usually does more for your DPS than maxing out Body alone. Keep an eye on your opponent too — if their board is lit up with multiple 50% markers, they're probably fishing for a reset. Bait the reset, then drop your own convergence on their cooldown window. That's Trinity Theory in a nutshell: patience beats power every time.
