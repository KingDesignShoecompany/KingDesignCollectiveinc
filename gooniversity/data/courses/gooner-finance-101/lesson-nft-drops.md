# NFT Drops

> **Course:** Gooner Finance 101 · **Lesson:** 3 of 3

## Minting Without Getting REKT

Right gooner, an NFT drop is basically a digital queue where you're paying gas fees to maybe get lucky, and the smart money treats it like a calculated gamble instead of a religious experience. The pre-game ritual starts days before the actual mint — you need to join the project's Discord, verify your wallet (they'll usually ask you to connect MetaMask), and cross your fingers that they don't implement a whitelist that favours their buddies. Whitelist spots are handed out to the most active members of their community or people who've been shilling their project for free, which means you either need to become best friends with the mods or accept that you're paying full price on launch day. The gooner approach is to study the rarity spreadsheet before mint, understand the utility, and most importantly have an exit plan.

## Reading Rarity and Utility

Rarity is the drug that gets gooners hooked, and the real ones know how to separate actual scarcity from marketing fluff. Before you mint, dig into the project's Discord and look for the rarity tool — sites like rarity.tools or nftgo.io will show you exactly how many of each trait exist across the collection. A trait that appears on 0.5% of tokens is genuinely rare, whereas something that only shows up because the devs forgot to add it to half the collection isn't scarcity, it's a bug. Utility is the harder sell — a project that promises "access to exclusive events" needs to deliver actual events, not just screenshots of a Discord channel nobody uses. The gooner who survives NFT drops is the one who mints early, sells a quarter of their allocation to cover gas, and holds the rest for the long game.

## Post-Mint Survival Tactics

Once you've actually minted, the real game begins — timing the market and not being a bag holder when sentiment shifts. The gooner rule is to list your cheapest mints immediately on OpenSea at a reasonable price, because floor sweeping bots will gobble up underpriced listings within minutes and relist them 20% higher. Watch the volume and the Discord chatter — if the mods have gone radio silent or the community is suddenly posting "diamond hands" memes, that's your signal that morale is tanking and you should exit your position. Don't get emotionally attached to jpegs, and always have a stop-loss mindset. The NFT space rewards the disciplined gooners who treat each drop like an investment thesis, not a lottery ticket, and who understand that the real alpha isn't getting in early — it's getting out at the right time.
