# Token Safety

> **Course:** Gooner Finance 101 · **Lesson:** 2 of 3

## The Rug Pull Reality Check

Look, the crypto space is basically the Wild West with better graphics, and the gooner survival rule is simple — if it sounds too good to be true, it's already stolen your money while you were reading this. A rug pull is when a project team quietly sells all their tokens and vanishes into the night, leaving you holding a worthless bag while their Twitter account goes radio silent. The red flags are usually obvious if you know where to look: no legitimate team reveals their real names, anonymous devs with anime profile pictures, liquidity that's less than 1% of the circulating supply, and audit reports that link to 404 pages. The gooners who survive are the ones who zoom out and ask "who benefits here" before throwing money at a whitepaper that reads like a fantasy novel.

## Contract Verification

The magic trick that saves more gooner wallets than any fancy trading strategy is learning how to read a contract on Etherscan or Solscan, and no it's not as scary as it sounds. Pull up the token contract address and look for a blue checkmark — that's usually a good sign, though not bulletproof since scammers have gotten sneaky with fake verification. Check the holders list: if 50% of the tokens are sitting in one wallet, that's your developer's wallet and they can dump at any time. The gooner shortcut is the holder distribution — you want to see thousands of small wallets, not a handful of whales who could wipe you out with a single sell order. If the project's too lazy to verify their source code, that's a hard pass. Code should be open, transparent, and boring.

## The Goon Scam Filter

Here's the system that's kept this gooner's portfolio intact through three bear markets and counting. First rule: never click random links in Telegram or Discord DMs, period. Second rule: any project promising guaranteed returns or "risk-free" yields is already operating on borrowed time. Third rule: when in doubt, don't buy — wait for the dust to settle and the charts to prove themselves. And the final rule that beats all others — if you're feeling FOMO because your mate just made 10x on something you've never heard of, that's exactly when you need to step away from your keyboard and go watch football instead. The gooner mentality is patient, research-first, and always questioning. The market will still be there tomorrow, but if you panic-buy into a honeypot smart contract, your money won't.
