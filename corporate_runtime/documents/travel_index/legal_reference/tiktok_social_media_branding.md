## 3 Pro-Tips for Launching "The KingDesignCollectiveINC"

### 1. The "Profile Video" Strategy
Pin a video to the top of your profile titled "What is The KingDesignCollectiveINC?"
- Show a 3-second clip on your couch
- Then a fast-paced montage of the 250 countries featured in your guide

### 2. Consistent Branding
Ensure your "Link in Bio" (whether using Linktree, Stan Store, or a personal site) is titled **The KingDesignCollectiveINC Shop** to match the username.

### 3. Watermarking
When posting cinematic travel clips, put a small, low-opacity text overlay that says `@TheKingDesignCollectiveINCIndex`. This prevents others from stealing your content and helps build brand recognition.
