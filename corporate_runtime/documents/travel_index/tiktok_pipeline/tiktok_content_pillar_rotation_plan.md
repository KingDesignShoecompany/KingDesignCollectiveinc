# TikTok Content Pillar Rotation Plan
## TheVagaryIndex
### Channels: Solo travel tips, Couple travel content, Guide promotions
Objective: convert top-of-funnel viewers into guide buyers and email subscribers.

## Core pillars
1. Solo travel tips
2. Couple travel content
3. Guide promotions

## 7-Day rotation
Day 1 - Solo
Day 2 - Couples
Day 3 - Guide Promo
Day 4 - Solo
Day 5 - Couples
Day 6 - Guide Promo
Day 7 - Solo / Couples mixed preview

## Format rules
- Hook: 0-3s benefit/stakes
- Body: 8-22s proof or tip
- CTA: 1s closing
- Branding: watermark @TheVagaryIndex
- CTA: Link in Bio = The Vagary Index Shop

## Solo travel tips
Hook: "One country, one wrong turn, one unforgettable night."
Content types: safety micro-tips, hostel hacks, solo food orders, transit shortcuts, single-room booking advice.
CTA: "See 250 country playbooks — link in bio."

## Couples travel content
Hook: "Fight less. Photograph more."
Content types: itinerary splits, romantic spots, compromise hacks, shared budget rhythms, photo spots.
CTA: "14-day couples itineraries inside — link in bio."

## Guide promotions
Hook: "Before you book anywhere, check this."
Content types: preview tiles, Albania sample, pricing reveal, bundle unboxing, reviewer testimony, map rotation.
CTA: "Grab the master pocketbook — link in bio."

## Country spotlight cadence
- ALB, FRA, JPN, MEX, KEN, AUS rotate weekly
- Each spotlight reuses one asset from real-country module
- Format: hook slide → climate/season slide → attraction/top10 slide → phrase card slide → itinerary tease → pricing CTA

## Performance QA
- Reply rate target > 3%
- Profile visits target > 6%
- Email capture target > 1.2%
- A/B test hook styles: transformation hook, authority hook, short & aesthetic
- Retain only formats with CTR > 2.5%
