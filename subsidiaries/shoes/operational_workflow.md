# OPERATIONAL WORKFLOW — SHOES
Brand: King Design Shoe Company
Supplier: AliveShoes (wholesale production)

## DAILY
- Review order queue (e-commerce + wholesale)
- Process supplier purchase orders with AliveShoes
- Update design backlog and asset library
- Respond to customer/wholesale account inquiries
- Track production and shipment status
- Log revenue and inventory movement

## WEEKLY
- Sales and margin review
- Wholesale account outreach
- Design iteration / new style planning
- Supplier performance and cost review

## EXCEPTIONS
On anomaly (supplier delay, quality issue, chargeback):
1. Halt related write operations
2. Flag to division lead
3. Record exception + expected impact
4. Activate fallback supplier or pause sales channel if needed

## KEY PROCESSES
1. Design → brand asset library
2. Customer/wholesale order → purchase order → AliveShoes
3. Production fulfillment → shipping → customer
4. Returns/quality → record → supplier claim or replacement
