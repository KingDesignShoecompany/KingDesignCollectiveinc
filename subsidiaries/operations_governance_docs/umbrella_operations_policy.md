# UMBRELLA OPERATIONS POLICY

## MODE
Local-first, sovereign, multi-agent operation under Hermes.

## OVERSIGHT
All subsidiaries adhere to:
- CEO authority over cross-entity decisions
- Autonomous operations within approved policies
- Full audit trail for all operations
- Secure inter-agent communication only

## ESCALATION CHAIN
Division Lead -> Sector Lead -> CEO -> Board (if applicable)

## APPROVAL RULES
- Capital movement: Finance + CEO
- IP changes: Innovation + Legal
- Structural changes: All division leads
